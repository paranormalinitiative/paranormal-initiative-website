## Purpose

The Member Help Center explains how to use The Paranormal Initiative: accounts,
profiles, community tools, media, research resources, privacy, safety,
policies, and reporting.

It is written for ordinary members. It must never expose internal moderation
procedures, staff credentials, role-appointment instructions, administrative
shortcuts, private member records, or production secrets.

The website experience is the member Help Center at `help-center.html`. Its
expandable topic groups use Facebook Help Center as an information-architecture
reference only. TPI wording, destinations, policies, examples, and safety
boundaries are original to TPI.

## Member support rules

### Account and access

- Members may create, sign in to, recover, and manage their own account.
- A missing staff, creator, contributor, or Administration Center link is an
  authorization boundary, not an invitation to try another account or direct
  URL.
- TPI support never needs a member password, recovery code, session cookie, or
  payment credential.

### Profiles and media

- Profile guidance covers the display name, short bio, avatar, background,
  honors, visibility, and the Profile editor.
- Photo and video guidance must distinguish public posts, profiles, Messenger,
  external links, and member-only media.
- A preview or external link is not an endorsement of the destination.
- Preserve original evidence separately from compressed, cropped, or annotated
  sharing copies.

### Community, privacy, and safety

- Separate direct observation from interpretation and speculation.
- Do not publish exact private addresses, private contact details, client
  information, private messages, or identifying media without permission.
- Explain the difference between a content report, a bug report, member
  support, blocking, and emergency assistance.
- TPI is not an emergency service. Direct immediate danger to appropriate local
  emergency or qualified professional help.

### Accessibility and troubleshooting

- Prefer keyboard access, browser zoom, current browsers, clear labels,
  captions, and direct media fallbacks.
- Ask for the page, control, browser, device, viewport, assistive technology,
  time, and visible error when a member reports a barrier.
- Never make clearing all browser storage the first troubleshooting step.
- Protect drafts, recordings, saved preferences, and member data during
  troubleshooting.

## Reporting routes

| Situation | Member route |
| --- | --- |
| Broken page, button, upload, preview, or layout | Report a bug |
| Harassment, threats, privacy violation, impersonation, or harmful content | Report abuse or a community concern |
| Login, recovery, profile, or account question | Member support |
| Immediate danger | Local emergency services or a qualified professional |
| Staff-only or administrative procedure | Do not expose it in this Help Center; route to authorized internal staff |

## Content maintenance

1. Check the current TPI interface and source before changing an article.
2. Keep the article title and sidebar label understandable without insider
   language.
3. Write in TPI’s evidence-first, privacy-aware voice.
4. Do not copy Facebook or another platform’s article text, screenshots, or
   product assumptions.
5. Update the article date and the release record when behavior changes.
6. Add a regression check when an article documents a permission, route, safety
   boundary, or other behavior that could regress.
7. Keep Pages and Groups deferred until their implementation is genuinely ready.

## Source and release boundary

The member UI data currently lives in `help-center-data.js`, rendered by the
shared Help Center shell. This Markdown file is the content and audience
reference for that UI. Changes to the member Help Center must preserve the
separation from `internal-help-center-data.js` and must pass the Help Center
separation regression before release.

The protected applications StudioFlow, Nova, and Creator Studio are unrelated
to ordinary member-help content and must remain byte-protected during a
Help Center release.
