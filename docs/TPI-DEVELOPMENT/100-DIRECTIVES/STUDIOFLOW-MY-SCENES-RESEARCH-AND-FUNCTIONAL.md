THE PARANORMAL INITIATIVE
STUDIOFLOW — MY SCENES RESEARCH AND FUNCTIONAL AUDIT
====================================================================

PRIORITY: HIGH
MODE: RESEARCH AND READ-ONLY INVESTIGATION

TARGET:
Existing production StudioFlow

https://paranormalinitiative.com/studio/

====================================================================
OBJECTIVE
====================================================================

Investigate how the My Scenes feature should operate.

The owner reports that the current scene controls
do not correspond to their labels.

Specifically:

Clicking Default Scene opens an upload prompt
requesting an outro video.

This is incorrect behavior.

We must establish the intended scene functionality
before implementing repairs.

====================================================================
1. RESEARCH STREAMYARD
====================================================================

Visit the official StreamYard website and Help Center.

Research:

- Scenes
- Scene switching
- Scene creation
- Scene editing
- Scene duplication
- Scene deletion
- Scene layouts
- Intro scenes
- Outro scenes
- Automatic intro/outro videos
- Media assets
- Backgrounds
- Saved scene configurations
- Scene persistence

Identify the distinction between:

A. Selecting a scene.

B. Editing a scene.

C. Assigning media to a scene.

D. Uploading media.

E. Automatically playing intro/outro videos.

Document how StreamYard actually handles these operations.

Do not assume TPI must copy every StreamYard feature.

====================================================================
2. SEARCH OUR HISTORICAL DIRECTIVES
====================================================================

Use the TPI historical directive knowledge base.

Search for:

"My Scenes"
"Scenes"
"Default Scene"
"Intro"
"Outro"
"Demo Scene"
"New Scene"
"Scene Switching"
"Scene Management"
"Scene Layout"
"Scene Persistence"

Recover all original requirements and subsequent changes.

Pay special attention to the final development-site
StudioFlow directives.

Determine whether our original scene design was intended
to resemble StreamYard's scene management.

Cite exact historical directive IDs and source locations.

====================================================================
3. INSPECT CURRENT MY SCENES
====================================================================

Inspect the existing production StudioFlow implementation.

Current visible scene controls reportedly include:

INTRO
DEFAULT SCENE
DEMO SCENE
DEMO SCENE 2
NEW SCENE
OUTRO

Determine what each control actually does.

Trace the click handlers and state changes.

Identify why selecting Default Scene opens
an outro-video upload prompt.

Check whether:

- Scene controls have incorrect event handlers.
- Scene IDs are mapped incorrectly.
- A shared upload control intercepts scene clicks.
- Scene selection and editing are confused.
- Scene content is associated with the wrong scene.
- Intro/outro upload behavior is incorrectly attached
  to general scene selection.
- Scene state is not properly restored.

Do not assume the root cause before inspecting the code.
