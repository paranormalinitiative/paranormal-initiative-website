## TPI-STUDIOFLOW-034 — ADDENDUM A
### VIDEO CLIP LABEL CORRECTION AND PLACARD SYSTEM

**OWNER DESIGN CORRECTION — REQUIRED**

1. Remove the permanent “Thank You” and “Be Right Back” labels from Video Clip slots 3 and 4.

2. Preserve all nine video slots:
   - Slot 1: Intro (protected).
   - Slot 2: Outro (protected).
   - Slots 3–9: Custom Video 3–9.

3. Video Clip slots 3–9 must support user-defined names.

4. Create a separate Placards section for professionally designed static presentation graphics.

5. Placards should support customizable text and visually attractive designs suitable for podcast production.

6. Include useful optional presets such as Starting Soon, Be Right Back, Thank You, and Technical Difficulties.

7. Placards must integrate with StudioFlow's existing scene and presentation system rather than creating a separate playback engine.

8. Preserve existing user-assigned media and scene references. Do not delete or overwrite assets previously assigned to slots 3 and 4.

9. Apply the same responsive-layout and visual-acceptance requirements established in Directive 034.

**The Video Clips section manages videos. The Placards section manages presentation graphics. These must be distinct, clearly organized features.**
