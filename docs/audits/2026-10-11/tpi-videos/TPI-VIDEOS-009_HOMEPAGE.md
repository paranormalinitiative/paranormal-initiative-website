# TPI-VIDEOS-009 Homepage

The TPI Videos homepage keeps the featured section, existing tabs, search, sort, source, category, and pagination controls. The legacy Continue Exploring and Community & Research cards were removed from this page. Format navigation now distinguishes Regular Videos from Shorts & Reels. The catalog refresh indicator is manual and non-interruptive: it does not reorder the current view until the member chooses to load new videos.
