# TPI-VIDEOS-003 Watch Pages

The existing watch page remains the detail surface for authored and approved discovered records. Provider embeds retain the existing provider validation and now use lazy loading, a strict referrer policy, and the established media permission list. External-only discovered records remain labeled and link to their source rather than being represented as TPI-hosted playback.

Channel identity includes a follow control when a creator channel exists. Recording, StudioFlow, and public live-video behavior were not changed.
