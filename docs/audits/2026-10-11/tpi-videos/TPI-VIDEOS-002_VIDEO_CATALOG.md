# TPI-VIDEOS-002 Video Catalog

The catalog keeps its source distinction:

- scraped provider records remain discovery records from existing ingestion;
- authored TPI records are contributor-owned and draft-first;
- public authored records appear only after explicit publication and allowed
  viewing access;
- provider, creator, and channel identity are shown where available.

Existing `tpi-videos.html` discovery and `tpi-video.html` watch surfaces were
extended only to display channel identity and preserve safe playback behavior.
There is no automatic provider import, public livestream launch, or StudioFlow
recording bridge in this batch.
