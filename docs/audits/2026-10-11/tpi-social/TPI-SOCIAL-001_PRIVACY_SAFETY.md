# Privacy and Safety

Server-side controls include sessions, member action restrictions, Messenger
blocks/restrictions, conversation membership, admin guards, owner checks, and
private-library ownership checks. Community moderation and reporting routes are
also present.

The repaired Messenger media gate closes the verified private-object exposure
for authenticated nonparticipants. Private-profile mode, expanded in-app
support forms, and all direct-link privacy combinations are not represented as
connected features and remain roadmap work.

Status: **PASS for tested gates; PARTIAL overall**.
