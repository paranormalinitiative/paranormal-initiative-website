# TPI-ADMIN-008 — Creator management

Existing creator partnership, Founding 50, Creator Studio entitlement, and
creator-channel APIs remain protected. The preview now includes a read-only
Creator Channels directory backed by the existing channel table, with
visibility/search metadata and published-video counts. It keeps channel
editing, ownership transfer, and lifecycle controls out of the preview until a
complete safe mutation contract is verified.

Founding 50 activation and Creator Studio entitlement grants were not
performed. No membership, entitlement, creator account, or channel record was
changed.
