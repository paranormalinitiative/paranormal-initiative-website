Introduction

I am publishing below two pieces taken from the ITC Journal, numbers 41 and 42. In the editorial note, I describe the study carried out by a radiophysicist impressed with the DRVs (direct radio voices) that I received from communicators who claimed to be speaking from a station in the next dimension of life called Rio do Tempo in my native Portuguese.
