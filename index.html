The Paranormal Initiative

Responsible research and investigation into the unexplained.

The Paranormal Initiative is a community for investigators, ITC and EVP researchers, experiencers, historians, builders, and curious people who want to take unusual experiences seriously without rushing to easy answers.

Start with the basics, go deeper when you are ready, and use the research areas, education material, repository, videos, and tools to learn, document carefully, ask better questions, and share knowledge responsibly.

Why This Exists

A professional home for research, education, standards, and shared knowledge.

The Initiative exists to help investigators, researchers, teams, educators, and the public explore unexplained experiences with better documentation, stronger evidence handling, clearer terminology, ethical field practice, and more responsible communication.

This is not meant to control how every team investigates. It is meant to build useful resources, preserve knowledge, support serious contributors, and encourage methods that reduce avoidable mistakes while keeping curiosity alive.

What We Do

Practical work for a complicated field.

Research & Field Methodology

Developing structured approaches to intake, walkthroughs, baselines, investigation, review, reporting, and responsible classification.

EVP, ITC & ACS Experimentation

Exploring audio and instrumental transcommunication methods through contamination awareness, source transparency, and careful interpretation.

Education & Investigator Development

Creating field papers, reference material, lessons, articles, and study areas for investigators at different experience levels.

Ethics & Evidence Integrity

Encouraging original-file preservation, careful claim language, witness respect, privacy, and honest communication of uncertainty.

Historical & Haunted Location Study

Connecting reported phenomena with local history, folklore, architecture, environmental context, and documented location research.

Technology & Documentation Tools

Building and organizing tools that support case management, field documentation, research review, education, and experimentation.

Collaboration

Built to grow beyond one perspective.

The foundation of The Paranormal Initiative is being built now so contributors, advisory members, investigators, researchers, historians, technicians, educators, experiencers, and teams can help expand the work over time.

The long-term goal is a contributor-based research and education platform where full papers, field notes, experiments, media, reference materials, and practical knowledge can be shared in an organized and professional way.
