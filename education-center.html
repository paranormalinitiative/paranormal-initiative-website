Research Library

The Paranormal Initiative Research Library is built for field papers and applied inquiry.

The Research Library hosts field papers, investigation development material, EVP/ITC experimentation, applied articles, method notes, and pathways through the repository.

If you are new here, think of this area as the library table: short articles, full papers, field notes, lessons, and research topics are organized so you can start simply and go deeper when you are ready.

How This Area Works

The Education Center is the publishing and study side of The Paranormal Initiative. Main navigation pages introduce major subjects; these shelves hold the papers, research notes, lessons, experiments, videos, and contributor materials connected to those subjects.

You can read casually, follow a topic, or use the material as a working reference during investigations.

Contributor Publishing

Approved contributors can use the Content Editor to draft full papers, save unpublished work, add media, and publish to the correct topic shelf.

Find What You Need

Use search when you already know the subject you want, or browse the shelves below when you want to explore by topic.
