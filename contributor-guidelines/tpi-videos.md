# TPI Video Writing Guide

A TPI Video is a video entry for The Paranormal Initiative video channel. Use this guide when creating or editing TPI video content.

## Required Fields

**Title**
Use the title shown on the original broadcast or a clear TPI program title.

**Video URL**
Paste the public Rumble, YouTube, or supported video page URL.

## Recommended Fields

**Description**
Summarize what viewers will see, hear, or learn in this video. Include the main topics, experiments, discussions, or presentations covered.

**Category**
Choose the subject that best matches the video:
- Applied Paranormal Research and Studies
- Live Streams
- EVP & ITC
- Visual ITC & Scrying
- Paranormal Investigation
- Equipment & Technology
- Research Discussion
- Education
- Special Presentation

## Optional Fields

**Embed URL**
Use the platform's approved embed URL when available. This allows the video to be embedded directly on the TPI Videos page.

**Thumbnail URL**
Choose an image that clearly represents the program or stream. If no thumbnail is provided, a branded fallback will be used.

**Tags**
Add useful search terms such as EVP, ITC, water scrying, investigation, or equipment. Separate tags with commas.

**Series**
Use this when the video belongs to a recurring TPI program.

**Episode Number**
Optional episode number for a recurring series.

**Duration**
Enter the approximate video length (e.g., 45:20 or 1:32:15).

**Featured**
When enabled, this video may be highlighted in the featured area of the TPI Videos page when no live stream takes priority.

**Live Stream**
Enable only while the broadcast is currently live. Live streams receive priority in the featured area and display a LIVE NOW indicator. When the stream ends, edit the entry and disable this option to keep the video as an archived recording.

## Publishing

1. Fill in the required fields (Title, Video URL)
2. Add a description and relevant details
3. Click **Publish Video**
4. The video will appear on the TPI Videos page automatically

## Editing Existing Videos

To edit a video after publication:
1. Open the video entry in the Content Editor
2. Make your changes
3. Click **Publish Video** to update

Common editing tasks:
- Disable "Live Stream" after a broadcast ends
- Update the thumbnail or description
- Add the video to a series
- Mark as featured

## Tips

- Use clear, descriptive titles that help viewers find content
- Write descriptions that explain what viewers will learn or see
- Add relevant tags to improve searchability
- Use consistent series naming for recurring programs
- Check that video URLs are publicly accessible
