# Sample Templates

These examples are starting points. Contributors can adjust headings when the subject needs a different structure, but every submission should remain clear, documented, and honest about limits.

## Research Paper Template

- Title
- Subtitle or short description
- Introduction
- Background or field context
- Key terms or definitions
- Main discussion
- Evidence, examples, sources, or observations
- Limitations
- Conclusion
- Author note
- References or source links

## Research Note Template

- Title
- Note type or topic
- Date or context if relevant
- Observation, question, or idea
- Why it matters
- Supporting details
- Limits or concerns
- Possible next steps
- Author note

## Experimental Report Template

- Title
- Experiment type
- Purpose or research question
- Date, time, and location if relevant
- Equipment or software used
- Setup
- Procedure
- Controls
- Observations
- Results
- Limitations
- Conclusion
- Suggested follow-up
- Author note

## Technical Note Template

- Title
- Tool, method, or system being discussed
- Plain-language summary
- What it does
- What it does not do
- Common uses
- Common mistakes
- Setup or workflow notes
- Limitations
- Example field language
- Author note

## Field Article Template

- Title
- Field topic or lesson
- What happened or what prompted the article
- Practical context
- What investigators should notice
- Documentation or safety concerns
- What this teaches
- Limitations
- Author note

## Review Paper Template

- Title
- Topic or question being reviewed
- Why the topic matters
- Sources, authors, methods, or ideas being compared
- Summary of major positions
- Strengths
- Weaknesses or limitations
- Open questions
- Conclusion
- References or source links
- Author note

## Case Or Location Study Template

- Title
- Location, case, or claim being studied
- Reason for study
- Historical or cultural background
- Reported claims
- Known sources and records
- Field observations if available
- Evidence or documentation reviewed
- Limitations
- Conclusion
- Author note

## Media Review Template

- Title
- Media type being reviewed
- Source, date, and file context
- Original-file status
- Review method
- Observations
- Possible contamination or ordinary explanations
- Classification
- Limitations
- Conclusion
- Author note
