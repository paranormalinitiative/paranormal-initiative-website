Note on the witnesses to my and other operators’ contacts

Professor Ernst Senkowski was the chief witness to the main ITC contacts that happened in the world until his death in 2015. They are well referenced and detailed in his major work Instrumentelle Transkommunikation from which I have been quoting (40). Dr Senkowski also witnessed some of my contacts on the occasions that he visited me.

Professor David Fontana watched and listened to Rio do Tempo voices on innumerable occasions at my houses in Lyon, France and in Vigo, Spain. In his book Is There an Afterlife? (chapter 14) he gives a detailed account of some of those (16).

Carlos Fernández (Fernández, 2006, 2020 among other works) was one of the three members of our initial little EVP experimental group and he not only witnessed the voices but interacted with them, also. In his book Voices from the Beyond (translated from the Spanish original), chapter 5 is about Rio do Tempo contacts (15).

Professor Uwe Hartmann A physicist, Dr Hartmann participated as an operator and scientist in the controlled experiments described in the NQ paper (7). Later, he visited me at my house in Galicia on a few occasions. He witnessed the DRV and even wrote an article about the measurements of the electromagnetic fields, which he performed at the Laboratory of Acoustics at the University of Vigo and in my recording studio during the occurrence of the voices (ITC Journal, issue 44-45, December 2012, pp. 26-30).

The operators Maria dos Anjos Antunes and Luisa Alcântara participated in the controlled experiments at the University of Vigo and the recording Studio Metropolis as described in the NQ paper (7). A few years before, in 2005, following my advice, they had carried out their own experiments at their houses and obtained results in the form of voices that identified as coming from Rio do Tempo and mentioned my name. Luisa Alcântara even published reports about her experiments in the ITC Journal, e.g., issue 30, 2007, pp. 57-61.
