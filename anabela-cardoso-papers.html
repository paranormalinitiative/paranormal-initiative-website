Major Contributor Collection

Anabela Cardoso Papers

A dedicated Education Center collection for Anabela Cardoso's work in EVP, ITC, psychical research, animal voice manifestations, radioelectric frequencies, and survival-hypothesis research.

These materials are being organized for The Paranormal Initiative with permission from Anabela Cardoso. Some documents require format conversion and translation review before final publication.

Major Contributor Profile EVP / ITC Topic Shelf

Collection Scope

This collection gathers Dr. Cardoso's papers and related materials for education, reference, and responsible study within the EVP and ITC research area.

The publication goal is to preserve full papers where permission allows, keep attribution clear, and provide translation notes where a paper uses mixed language.

Current Status

Duplicate downloads have been collapsed into a working source set. Each record below links to the staged source document; the next pass can convert each file into a polished readable HTML paper and add translation notes where needed.
