Instrumental TransCommunication (ITC) – a Transpersonal Connection Anabela Cardoso

Lic. in Germanic Philology (Master's degree) Doctor in Public Service (Honoris Causa) ITC Journal Research Centre anabela@itcjournal.org

Abstract The author introduces EVP/ITC, the method to attempt the contact with unknown dimensions of life through electronic devices. As early as the 19th century, hints about the advent of EVP (Electronic Voice Phenomena), the popular form of what we currently call Instrumental Transcommunication (ITC), were received on Earth. Anomalous electronic contacts started early in the 20th century via the telephone but it is the renowned Swedish artist Friedrich Jürgenson who is rightly called “the father of electronic voices”. The Latvian philosopher and psychologist Dr Konstantin Raudive is the most prominent figure among his followers. The end of the 20th century saw incredible developments in the field with the appearance of other forms of electronic communication, namely video images and computer texts. The recipients of these advanced contacts, which yielded hundreds of pages of mindboggling information, were the great transcommunicators of Luxembourg and Germany. The German Professor of Physics Ernst Senkowski followed and studied their work painstakingly and published comprehensive reports about the phenomena he witnessed. To conclude, the author describes her experiences in ITC and the current state of her research.

Key words: EVP, DRV (Direct Radio Voices), ITC (Instrumental Transcommunication), electronic voices, Schreiber method, transpartners. ____________________________________________________________

The announcements Electronic Voice Phenomena (EVP), later called ITC by the German physicist Ernst Senkowski to comprise other forms of electronic communication besides voices, is a new method allegedly devised by scientists in the next dimension of life, to contact our physical world and enable us to attempt communication with our deceased loved ones and other entities in that dimension, via electronic means. By the middle of the 19th century, Allan Kardec, the brave French scholar who codified Spiritism in its erudite version, avowed that the way of communicating with the world of the deceased would radically change in the future. Until then, human mediumship had been the vehicle that purportedly conveyed the messages from the dead. I quote from The First Book of the Spirits: Question 468: Is not the loss of those who are dear to us a legitimate source of sorrow, seeing that this loss is both irreparable and independent of our action? Reply: This cause of sorrow, which acts alike upon rich and poor, is the common law of humanity, for it is either a trial or an expiation; but you have the consolation of holding communication with your friends through the means already possessed by you, that develop further every day, while awaiting other means that will be more direct, and more accessible to your senses. And in Kardec’s Revue Spirite, I found the following passage: ... Later on, electricity will make its mediumistic revolution, and since everything will then be changed in the way of reproducing the thought of the Spirit you will no longer find such defaults, which are sometimes regrettable especially when the communications are read in front of strangers (Kardec, 1864). Later, already in the 20th century, other mediums predicted the same method. In 1936, publisher Paul Leymarie released Suzanne Max-Getting’s Messages by a Liberated Spirit (Grandsire, 1998). The 26th of these messages, of February 2nd, 1930, announces: … the Spiritualist movement is beginning to spread. The role of mediums in the future…We will speak to you through the medium of the phonograph and you will see us on film… the medium will play a passive role in the future. … In many cases the devices will replace the work of the mediums … We will thus do without the mediums through the phonograph………………………………………………………………….. The beginning Since the middle of the 20th century, our civilization has rapidly evolved into a technological society based primarily on electronics. And with electronics, the way the so-called spirits communicate with us changed as Allan Kardec had already prognosticated. Instrumental Transcommunication (ITC) is the latest development in the communication between dimensions. But anomalous electronic communications were reported much earlier via the telephone. Oscar D’Argonnel, the meticulous Portuguese-Brazilian investigator who applied experimental research to the study of the paranormal phenomena of Spiritism, was apparently the first to describe and report on the anomalous telephone calls (D’Argonnel, 1925). Years later, purely by chance, the catholic priests and scientists, Fathers Gemelli and Ernetti, were the first to record electronic voices; The American photographer and medium Attila von Szalay, in collaboration with Raymond Bayless, followed. However, these initial occurrences did not reach far because the voice phenomena remained isolated and ignored. Although there were others who previously recorded electronic voices, we may rightly call Friedrich Jürgenson the great pioneer of the voice phenomenon. He discovered EVP on the 12th of June, 1959, by chance, while recording the songs of night birds, and never knew that other investigators had recorded electronic voices before him even if in sporadic occurrences. A famous artist of his time, privileged to see some of his paintings hanging in the Museum of the Vatican alongside those of Michelangelo, a singer who performed at La Scala in Milan, self-proclaimed atheist confidant of the two Popes of his time, Pius XII and Paul VI, Jürgenson achieved the unthinkable. His intelligence, sensitivity and commitment prompted him to spread the good news to the world. He spared no difficulties or efforts, but dedicated his entire life and career to the noble mission of opening the path of understanding to humanity. For the first time, objective communication was achieved with an unknown dimension of life from where the dead could speak with the Earth. However, regrettably, the great pioneer’s heritage has not been duly cherished and honoured. Orthodox, uninformed parapsychologists contributed to this state of affairs by dismissing the evidence without studying and learning about it from the beginning. Although there are exceptions, they usually base their mistrust on futile claims of anomalous electronic voices posted online by uncritical or unscrupulous operators. The latter are normally frivolous or misguided experimenters who pursue what they call ghost hunting with disastrous results. Jürgenson himself advised against this mess that darkened his last days in our world. Already during his lifetime, he became disturbed by the fact that “in his footsteps followed charlatans, quacks, fanatical occultists and the like, who utilized his discovery and just minted coins from it” as his close friend and admirer Anders Leopold reported (Cardoso & Leopold 2023). Friedrich Jürgenson’s books were published in Swedish and in German but there is a recent English translation (Jürgenson, 2004). The most illustrious of Jürgenson’s followers was Dr Konstantin Raudive, a Latvian philosopher and intellectual, disciple of Carl Gustav Jung, who taught at the University of Uppsala, Sweden. With published work to his credit, he managed to assemble around him and his work on electronic voices, scientists from prestigious European institutions. Professor of Physics Alex Schneider of St. Gallen University, Prof Dr Gebhard Frei, Theodor Rudolph, high-frequency electronics engineer at Telefunken Co, Dr Ing. Franz Seidl, who invented the Psychophone as well as a special hearing device which allowed the deaf, even by birth, to hear, and the renowned German parapsychologist Professor Hans Bender, to name a few, are some of the top European experts in the field of physics and electronics that collaborated with Raudive. Some also developed their own voice experiments successfully. Raudive’s book about the voice phenomena, Unhörbares wird Hörbar, was published in Germany in 1968. The English translation, entitled Breakthrough, an amazing experiment in electronic communication with the dead shook the world and encouraged thousands of people to experiment with electronic voices. Raudive’s excellent book was republished by White Crow Books recently. Later, other experimenters followed in Jürgenson’s and Raudive’s footsteps and obtained good results. The great transcommunicators In the late 1980’s, Maggy and Jules Harsch-Fischbach from Luxembourg and Adolf Homes from Rivenich, Germany, stand out among other important researchers from around the world such as Peter Härting (Senkowski, 1995, 2000) and the Italian Marcello Bacci (Bacci, 1991). Together with those of Adolf Homes, their results are the most impressive in the history of what was already called Instrumental Transcommunication (ITC). The new discipline comprises all forms of paranormal communications received through electronic devices – EVP voices that cannot be heard directly but only when rewinding the recording, Direct Radio Voices (DRV), which can be heard by anyone present in the room, anomalous images and computer texts, ‘phone calls and other messages such as those received through mobile phones that are currently reported by many people. The famous contacts of Luxembourg and Rivenich were followed, studied and compiled by Ernst Senkowski in his major work Instrumentelle Transkommunikation (Senkowski, 2000). The German physicist and the Italian Dr Eng. Carlo Trajna are rightly considered the most knowledgeable academics in the field of ITC. Both scientists carried out their own experiments with success. Other scientists and researchers from different regions of the globe witnessed the contacts of the Harsch-Fischbach and of Adolf Homes, and even interacted with the communicating entities (Senkowski, ibid). The operators were the recipients of mindboggling information and wisdom purportedly imparted by high beings. Maggy was a school teacher and Jules Harsch-Fischbach a Luxemburgish lawyer who worked for the Government while Adolf Homes was an unassuming carpenter who had difficulty understanding most of the information he received and often asked Senkowski to clarify its meaning. The Luxembourgish operators called the high entity that directed their contacts, the ‘Technician’. This enigmatic being of extraordinary knowledge and wisdom avowed: “I am not a being of energy nor a being of light, I am not human, I am not an animal, I have never been incarnated; I am of a completely different nature and I am not God either” (Locher and Harsch, 1989, 1992, 1995). In her book, written in collaboration with Dr Theo Locher, the President of the Swiss Parapsychological Association, Maggy Harsch reproduces some of the many conversations with the Technician and the ethical advice received from the latter (Locher and Harsch 1989, 1992, 1995; Senkowski, 2000). Adolf Homes’ amazing computer texts Besides his monumental work, sometimes called the ‘Bible of ITC’, Senkowski collected Homes’ messages in a special, separate publication entitled Transkommunikation, Vol 4 (German original, 1999). There is an Italian translation of the same work Transcomunicazione (Numero Speciale, 1999). Often, the texts appeared spontaneously on Adolf Homes’ computer screen, mainly in reply to questions previously put to the communicators by Senkowski and others. Some of those questions had been written on a piece of paper at the German physicist’s house in Mainz but not communicated to Homes who lived in Rivenich. Nevertheless, the replies appeared on Homes’ computer screen or diskette making reference to Senkowski’s queries (Senkowski, ibid). I transcribe below a few sentences, randomly taken from Homes’ computer texts, which illustrate the anti-paradigm, non-anthropocentric content of ITC messages received from non-human entities who claimed to be high beings of a different nature. “... All that lives, and everything lives, is part of creation. Concepts live, the dead live, computers live, everything lives. Every life is fulfilled through transformation. Every life in the worlds, even the most diverse, searches [for] unlimited perfection ...”. And “... Every life – animal, plant, mineral, and all kinds of coarse matter as well as subtle matter – consists solely and absolutely of information”. Or, “Our request to you is that of identifying yourselves with your planet and all life in it because everything of you is formed of identical elementary particles. To us silicon, nerve cell and consciousness are absolutely identical...” (Senkowski, 1999, German original p. 49; Italian translation, 1999, p. 132; Cardoso 2017, p. 19). Over 200 long computer texts were received by Homes and analysed by Senkowski and his colleague Dr Vladimir Delavre. Overall, the teachings received from high entities insist that spiritual progress is not connected to any religion or spiritual practice but rather to the respect for Nature and every life form, as well as to the development of inner consciousness. The Technician declared in Luxembourg: “The respect for each living being is part of the upcoming spiritual development of man”. And added, “In the future ITC should encompass human rights and the protection of animals and of Nature” (Locher and Harsch, 1989, 1992, 1995). Also in Luxembourg, a deceased French scientist intruded into the established DRV contact, and delivered an astounding direct speech. I quote Senkowski’s report on the unexpected occurrence. “In the presence of seven persons (B.W., J.P.S., Meek, Resch, Senkowski), Henry Sainte Claire Deville made contact on May 2nd, 1987, 8 p.m., as the speaker of transgroup LIFELINE (MTC partner of American METASCIENCE). He spoke in English with conspicuous French accent, directly via the loudspeaker of a USW receiver of the ESB within the 90 MHz range; intelligibility 100%. Investigations showed that drop-in communicator Deville, unknown to all those present, had been an excellent French chemist of the 19th century who, among others, was the first to study the properties of crystalline silicon. The year 1881, as that of his passing, is correct. Full transcription of the original: “My name is Henry Sainte Claire Deville. I listened to this message on a copy of the original audio, which Senkowski kindly gave me. The question of God has preoccupied humanity since ancient times. The higher entities who spontaneously or in response to questions from Senkowski and other scientists, broke into Adolf Homes' computer, approached the subject from two different angles. On the one hand, there is what they call the “Divinity” or the “God of the Origins”, “the Principle” that is, the source and totality of all that exists. The source, or the principle, cannot be described or understood neither by us nor by our transpartners, even the higher beings in question. God is all that is and is beyond any possibility of expression or comprehension. There is nothing else to say. On the other hand, also according to the messages, there are the “pseudo gods” or “secondary gods”, i.e. the gods of all religions, created by man to serve his own purposes and very much the result of the human fear of death. Those anthropomorphic gods have manipulated to disturb the divine order of the origins, the “Truth”, as the communicators also call the “God of the Origins”. Much remains to be said about the transcendental information received by Maggy and Jules Harsch-Fischbach and by Adolf Homes from superior beings of non-human nature. However, this article focuses primarily on my own experiences in an area that I never thought would become my main interest to the point of radically changing my life as it did. My personal involvement I have been experimenting with Instrumental Transcommunication (ITC) with good results since the beginning of 1998. At first, we were a small group of three friends and we started experimenting with visual ITC using the Schreiber method. Klaus Schreiber was the German experimenter who spread the word about the capture of the transimages – paranormal images that appear on a TV or computer screen, spontaneously or upon demand by the operator. Schreiber, reportedly under the guidance of his deceased daughter Karin, discovered the feedback loop method, also known as the Schreiber method – The operator connects a video camera to a television without images, or to a computer screen without streaming or Internet, in a closed circuit. The screen will reflect what the video camera captures. Unstable, oscillating light spots normally form, and the video camera will project the filming back again into the screen in a feedback loop. The short video (of no more than a few minutes) must be examined afterwards, frame by frame, and a transimage might appear. Apparently, the method allowed the German pioneer to obtain post- mortem images of his deceased family members. Schreiber’s work was closely followed by Rainer Holbe, a reputable German journalist who published extensively about it (Holbe, 1987). Senkowski describes his close contacts with Klaus Schreiber, as well as some of Schreiber’s results, in his previously mentioned work, Instrumentelle Transkommunikation (pp. 70-72, 294-295). We obtained interesting results from the beginning (at the very end of 1997) but I decided that the transimages we got, although good, did not have the photographic quality I aimed for. They were recognizable, although slightly blurred. I aimed at credible evidence from the very beginning of my ITC involvement. After all, this practice is supposed to be the experimental demonstration that life continues after physical death… Carl Sagan popularized the well-known saying, “Extraordinary claims require extraordinary evidence”, and I must say that I abide by it. The first transimage we received very much resembled the deceased Dona Dae, as I called the aged harlequin Great Dane female dog I had rescued and taken care of a couple of years before, after finding her dying of hunger and carelessness on the streets of the village near my house. The transimage clearly reflected the black and white spots on her head as well as her pointed ears. We received several other transimages, although perhaps less identifiable. But one of them is of special value and deserves special mention. I called it ‘the Mask’ because it seems to depict a mask. However, the most interesting thing about this image, which I obtained while experimenting alone at home, is the circumstance that when I asked Rio do Tempo why they had sent me the image, they replied, “The mask is our way to tell you that our world unveils the truth” (literal translation). This happened later when I was already receiving DRV, at a time when I was very preoccupied with the meaning of the “Truth” from an existential, ethical and philosophical perspective, and used to frequently ask my communicators about the existence of “Absolute Truth”. Typically, in EVP experiments, the operator sits in silence and asks questions, at a regular pace, of potential invisible communicators. If suddenly, the radio answers out loud one of those questions and starts talking to you, as happened to me, words cannot describe the surprise, the emotion and, above all, the shock it produces in a layperson as was my case. In ITC terminology, EVP are voices which are not audible when the experimenter makes his or her recordings but during playback only. Conversely, the DRV are voices that come into the air directly from the loudspeaker of a radio and which may reply to the Earth operator’s questions, thus allowing for a dialogue. From that day of March 1998, until today, I never stopped working in ITC. My enthusiasm for these mysterious voices was such that I sacrificed my personal and professional life to the needs of this mind-boggling quest. Perhaps because having no experience or knowledge of so-called paranormal phenomena, I considered DRV to be virtually impossible until one day I experienced them personally. DRV allowed me to objectively communicate with an invisible dimension of life and ask some of my beloved deceased relatives, as well as unknown people, who claimed to speak from another world, about their living conditions. This was the case of Carlos de Almeida, my main communicator, who seemed to be in charge of the contacts and of establishing the so-called ‘Bridge’ that he specifically mentioned, as well as directing the flow of DRV I received almost daily. He spoke with me for a couple of years, and introduced other people, namely some of my family members. I believe he helped them to communicate. I was thrilled and rejoiced every single time I turned on my radios and his familiar voice echoed in the room. This was bliss! I never experienced such a happy time in my adult life. But suddenly, one evening, he told me that he “was ready to leave [his world] and move on [to a more advanced level]”. Since then, he never spoke with me again. I truly miss him - his kind, cheerful but assertive manner and his sense of humour. AUDIO 2. The ITC work required immense dedication and it became a full-time job if I wanted to do it well, because I received many voices and they require a lot of work. That was the reason why I retired before completing my time in the diplomatic career - so that I could reciprocate the efforts of my communicators which enabled the ITC results I got. But “it was all worth it”, as a voice that identified itself as belonging to my deceased father, answered me one day when I asked him if the happiness he enjoyed in his new life in the other world was worth the hardships he had endured during his life on Earth. Gradually, our little group of three people separated and I was the only one who carried on experimenting, always with great enthusiasm and elation. Of the three, I was also the only one really interested in the phenomenon. That was when I contacted Professor Ernst Senkowski for the first time and told him what was happening. He was welcoming, understanding and enthusiastic. We kept in close contact until a few years ago, when his long life in this world ended. I owe a lot to this brilliant academic, who generously shared with me his deep knowledge and experience in the field of ITC. His wisdom, intelligence and profound erudition left an imprint on me that has lasted ever since. Ernst Senkowski was the master and friend any ITC operator aspires to. My gratitude to him is endless. A tsunami of voices For many years, at my house in Galicia, Spain, as well as at my house in Lyon, France, and again in Galicia to where I returned at the end of my diplomatic mission in France, DRV happened on a scale that I could never have dreamt of. Particularly in my first two books (2010, 2017), I reported on the most significant content of the DRV I received and compared it to the information obtained by other reputable ITC operators, mainly, but not only, Maggy and Jules Harsch-Fischbach from Luxembourg and Adolf Homes from Rivenich, Germany. In these works, I mention many of the topics that, in general, concern humanity: God, suffering, reincarnation, the meaning of life, death, love, ethics and others. The most important characteristic that I found in the transcendental communications that I examined, was the concurrence of information obtained by ITC operators from different parts of the globe, and even from different times, who had never met or talked to each other. Nevertheless, they say basically the same thing… Stations in the next world My communicators claim that they speak from a Station - a concept already transmitted to Friedrich Jürgenson and from him onwards- and called it Rio do Tempo. According to the information received from our transpartners, as Senkowski called them, those stations, or communication centres transmit from their dimension to ours and other physical worlds. Only some have access to the stations, and we should not fear malevolent interventions from the voices that originate therein, namely the DRV, because the so-called ‘bad spirits’ are not allowed to communicate from there. When possible, I mean when the contact ran easily, something that did not always happen, I asked my communicators all kinds of questions about existential matters, and obtained responses to a number of them. One very important requisite for these advanced contacts (the ones transmitted from a station) to take place, seems to be the indispensable permission not only to communicate with a certain person on Earth, but also to answer questions about specific issues, which we cannot identify in advance. A conspicuous example of this situation happened in the 1920s when the Brazilian researcher Oscar D’Argonnel (ibid, 1925) was repeatedly informed by the entities that communicated with him through the telephone (I quote and translate): “… I have already told you [D’Argonnel] that here, we cannot do anything without higher permission…” (p. 45). And, “We have strict laws here and today I am not allowed to chat with her [his wife]” (p. 21). This little book, the first to report on the anomalous telephone conversations, as I pointed out above, is a jewel for any serious ITC researcher. In general, all the aspects of those extraordinary telephone contacts of 100 years ago bear a great resemblance to the current ITC ones, both from the point of view of the acoustic characteristics of the voices and the content of the messages. In Luxembourg and at Adolf Homes’, messages were subject to fewer restrictions, perhaps because they were devised and implemented directly by higher beings. According to our communicators, the expansion of human consciousness is the main purpose of our earthly life. A French researcher, architect Pierre Théry, reported on the first telephone call from the deceased Konstantin Raudive to a lady in France, Mme Aline Piget. During his reasonably long conversation, Dr Raudive told Mme Piget: “…. I would like you to know, dear Aline, that the object of an earthly life is not just the goodness. The object is to be conscious...” (ITC Journal, 2, p. 42-43). I had the opportunity of exchanging views with Pierre Théry about this important event and of discussing with him its authenticity, which he fully supported. Mme Aline Piget knew about ITC since the death of her beloved son. She had had contacts with him through the French EVP pioneer Monique Simonet (Simonet, 1991, 2004) but she never expected a telephone call from Konstantin Raudive! Faced with some doubts from her friends about the genuineness of Raudive's voice, Mrs. Piget requested an analysis from Pr G. Ferrandi, a proficient French acoustic engineer. His study involved computer sonograms and established that he was "convinced of the presence of a paranormal voice". Konstantin Raudive was one of the regular communicators in Luxembourg and at Rivenich. He counselled humanity on ethical values and behaviour. EVP experiments under controlled conditions Some years ago, I was invited to devise and direct a research project on the anomalous electronic voices. My proposal intended to emulate the EVP tests carried out in England with Dr Raudive before the publication of his Breakthrough as described by Peter Bander (1972). The experiments took place at the Acoustics Laboratory of the Faculty of Engineering of the University of Vigo, Spain, under the strictest and most controlled conditions currently available. The internationally renowned acoustic expert and recording studio designer Philip Newell supervised the tests. The results of the investigation were published in NeuroQuantology, a peer-reviewed journal, under the title ‘A Two-Year Investigation of the Allegedly Anomalous Electronic Voices or EVP’ (NeuroQuantology | September 2012 | Volume 10 | Issue 3 | Page 492-514). Anomalous voices were recorded in most of the many sessions. On page 497 of the report, I peruse the circumstances of a DRV that mentions the deceased Raudive’s presence in the professional recording studio where the experiment took place. Scholars interested in psychic phenomena would benefit from reading this report, which, together with Professor Hans Bender’s testing of Jürgenson’s voices (Bender, 1970, 2011), is the only study on anomalous electronic voices published in a scientific journal.
