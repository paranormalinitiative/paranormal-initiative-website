Software Use Terms

Free ITC and EVP research software from The Paranormal Initiative.

The Paranormal Initiative develops software tools to support research, education, documentation, EVP, ITC, visual ITC, field investigation, and paranormal experimentation. These tools are made available free of charge, but they are not open-source software.

Our Apps Third-Party Notices

Permitted Use

Users may install and use TPI software for personal, educational, field, research, documentation, EVP, ITC, and paranormal experimentation purposes. The software is intended to help researchers organize, observe, document, compare, and review material with greater structure.

TPI tools are experimental research aids. They do not prove spirit communication, paranormal activity, survival, or any specific interpretation of recorded or observed material. Users remain responsible for careful documentation, ethical use, contamination awareness, and independent review.
