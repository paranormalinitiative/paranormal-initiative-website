Software Use Terms

Research software from The Paranormal Initiative.

The Paranormal Initiative develops software tools to support research, education, documentation, EVP, ITC, visual ITC, field investigation, and paranormal experimentation. TPI software may be provided as browser-based applications or downloadable software, depending on the project. Access and availability may vary by application.

Our Apps Third-Party Notices

Permitted Use

Users may access and use TPI software for personal, educational, field, research, documentation, EVP, ITC, and paranormal experimentation purposes, subject to the access terms provided for each application.

TPI software is designed to help researchers organize, observe, document, compare, review, and evaluate experimental material with greater structure.

Researchers remain responsible for careful documentation, ethical use, contamination awareness, experimental controls where appropriate, and the interpretation of their own observations and findings.
