Artificial Intelligence

AI should support research and education without replacing human judgment.

Artificial intelligence is becoming part of research, writing, education, media review, documentation, and public communication. Some people are excited by it, some are cautious, and some are concerned about what it may change. The Paranormal Initiative treats AI as a tool that requires transparency, boundaries, and responsible use.

AI can help organize information, explain concepts, suggest reading paths, support research notes, and make educational material easier to navigate. It should not authenticate evidence, declare paranormal conclusions, replace investigator judgment, or create false certainty.

Research Support

AI can help summarize notes, organize sources, compare terminology, draft outlines, and point users toward relevant site materials.

Education & Clarity

AI can explain methods, equipment, EVP, ITC, investigation workflow, haunted location research, and responsible review language.

Documentation Help

AI can assist with report structure, field-note organization, readability, and consistency while preserving the investigator's original observations.

Disclosure & Safeguards

AI-assisted writing, graphics, or research support should be labeled when appropriate, checked against sources, and reviewed by a human.

Evidence Boundaries

AI should not be used to manufacture evidence, over-enhance media, invent sources, or turn an unresolved observation into a paranormal conclusion.

The TPI Position

Artificial intelligence can be useful when it improves access to knowledge, organization, and education. It becomes harmful when it creates false confidence, hides uncertainty, or replaces careful human review.
