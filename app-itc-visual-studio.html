Visual ITC Research

ITC Visual Studio

ITC Visual Studio is a free visual experimentation workspace for researchers exploring water, steam, reflective surfaces, light movement, image review, and visual ITC session documentation. The goal is careful capture, comparison, and review rather than automatic interpretation.

Research Position

The app supports visual experimentation and review. It does not label images as evidence or make paranormal claims for the user.
